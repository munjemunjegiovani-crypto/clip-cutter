4. Counter-Notification & Repeat Infringers

reply Counter-Notice Protocol

If an account holder believes an active task was seized in error or misidentification (e.g. fair use or licensed stock), they may submit a formal counter-notification under 17 U.S.C. § 512(g)(3). Upon receipt of a valid counter-notice, ClipCutter promptly delivers a copy to the claimant. Unless the claimant files an active court order within 10-14 business days, access may be restored if files have not already self-purged.

block Three-Strike Infringer Policy

In compliance with federal guidelines, ClipCutter enforces a strict repeat infringer termination rule. Accounts subject to three valid, unchallenged DMCA takedown notices within a rolling 12-month period will have their rendering privileges and ClipCutter Pro licenses permanently revoked.
