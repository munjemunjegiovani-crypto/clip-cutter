Architectural Privacy by Design

ClipCutter does not operate as long-term video asset storage or cloud archive hosting. From initial upload chunking to optical action detection, our processing environment is isolated within ephemeral NVMe RAM caches and encrypted scratch volumes. Raw user footage is never stored in persistent, permanent multi-tenant cloud buckets.

memory Zero Model Training on Footage

We maintain an immutable operational guarantee: ClipCutter never feeds user video, audio spectrograms, actor faces, voiceprints, or motion heatmaps into machine learning model training loops or foundation dataset weights.

lock Local Buffer & TLS 1.3 Ingestion

Footage ingress operates through high-throughput, TLS 1.3 encrypted sockets directly to runtime worker nodes. All in-transit packets are decoded into volatile scratch disks using hardware AES-256-XTS envelope encryption keys.

24-Hour Cryptographic Shredding SLA

Every uploaded media frame, cut sequence, audio stem, and intermediate proxy file is tagged with a hardware timestamp upon ingress. Exactly 24 hours post-ingestion, our autonomous cryptographic daemon shreds the allocated partition keys. Once the purge completes, neither ClipCutter support engineers, cloud administrators, nor automated algorithms can inspect, reconstruct, or retrieve your media.

Global Creator Privacy Rights

Whether you are an independent video editor in Europe, North America, or across the globe, we provide consistent, unified digital rights under GDPR (Articles 15-22), California Consumer Privacy Act / CPRA (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.100), and Canadian PIPEDA.

Right to Access & Portability

Request a machine-readable JSON export of your account balance, transaction receipts, and processing logs within 7 business days.

Right to Erasure (RTBF)

Obliterate all records tied to your email identifier. Once authorized, our pipeline executes complete removal across active databases and backups.

Absolute Prohibition of Sale

ClipCutter has never sold, leased, or rented personal consumer data or raw media footage to third parties. We do not maintain an ad exchange.

Cross-Border Cloud Protection

All international data transfers comply strictly with the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and UK International Data Transfer Addendum.
