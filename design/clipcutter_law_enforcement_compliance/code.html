SECTION 03 //

18 U.S.C. § 2703(f) Preservation Requests

ClipCutter complies with official requests from sovereign law enforcement agencies to preserve records pursuant to 18 U.S.C. § 2703(f) pending the issuance of formal legal process.

lock_clock 90-Day Retention Lock

Upon receipt of a valid, signed 2703(f) letter, ClipCutter will isolate and preserve applicable data in our custody for a period of 90 days, renewable for one additional 90-day extension upon written notification.

history_toggle_off Point-in-Time Scope

Preservation orders are strictly prospective snapshots of data existing at the millisecond of receipt. A preservation order cannot undelete or recreate media already shredded by the 24-hour autonomous cryptographic purge.

Mandatory Formalities for 2703(f) Letters:
  • Issued on official agency letterhead signed by a sworn detective, special agent, or assistant prosecuting attorney.
  • Explicit identifier specifying the ClipCutter account email, session hash, or Stripe transaction token.
  • Explicit declaration that steps are being taken to obtain a court order or warrant within the statutory 90-day window.
